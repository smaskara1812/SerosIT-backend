Notes:
- "new-system table" entries above (sys_*, mst_user_password,
  company_to_location_mapping, mst_company_loc_type/ownership) have no
  1:1 legacy row data — seed or leave empty, don't try to map legacy rows
  onto them. mst_company_loc_type/ownership DO need their handful of
  lookup values seeded (Office/Hypermart/Plant, Essar/Aegis/etc.) but as
  a small manual seed, not a bulk migration.
- Before writing the real migration script, grep each model's docstring
  in backend/core/models.py for "New table" / "no legacy" / "straight
  copy of legacy X" — this codebase has been documenting that distinction
  per-model throughout the build, so it's the fastest way to confirm which
  of the 92 need real legacy data vs a fresh/empty start.
